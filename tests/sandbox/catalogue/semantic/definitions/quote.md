A **quote** is a priced offer (`quotes`, from Pipedrive, which confusingly calls them deals).
`quote_value_eur` is ex-VAT. Every quote carries a final status - Won, Lost or Expired - and a
`decided_date`; there is no open status, so "open quotes" cannot be counted from this table.
Quotes exist for Finland only.
