The **budget** is the plan the board approved in December 2025. It does not move. It was built at
the planning rate 10.80 EUR/SEK, while actuals convert at the monthly average, so Bergström's
budget variance always carries an FX component. The budget is not the target: targets are revised
and live in the planning sheet.
