---
metric: utilisation
summary: "Technician hours booked on work orders over 160 available hours per technician-month."
about: technician_utilisation_monthly.utilisation_rate
aliases: [utilisation, utilization, technician utilisation, capacity used]
---
**Utilisation** = hours booked on work orders / (160 x technician-months) over the asked window.
Above 100 % means more than 160 hours booked. Computed from the monthly mart; never average the
per-row `utilisation` column.
