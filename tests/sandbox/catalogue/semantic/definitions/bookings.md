A **booking** is a signed contract: the Compass deal's contract value, dated by close date.
Bookings are not revenue - revenue arrives when invoiced, usually across several invoices.
Partner-registered deals are included (they are a subset of all bookings, never an addition).
`legacy_marts.mart_sales_bookings_v1` counted quotes sent and is frozen; do not use it.
