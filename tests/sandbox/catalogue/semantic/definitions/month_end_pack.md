The **month-end pack** is the set of figures group finance closed after month end. Once sent it
never changes. Two things make it differ from today's books: invoices booked after the close
(about one in 25 contracting invoices; see `revenue_restatements`), and Bergström converted at
the **closing** rate rather than the average rate the books use. A "what did we report" question
reads the pack; a "what is revenue" question reads the current books.
